Precautions

General Precautions and Safe Laboratory Practices

The instructions under the above mentioned category are listed below:

  • Access to laboratory area must be restricted to authorized personal only.
  • Staff must wear white coat on entering the laboratory and remove it before leaving the laboratory.
  • Keep laboratory clean and tidy and retain only necessary items.
  • Do not store food or personal items in the laboratory, especially in the main area of the laboratory.
  • Wear gloves while collecting and handling specimens and remove before leaving the workplace.
  • Do not touch your own skin, mucous membranes, electric switches, door knob, register etc. with gloved hands.
  • Wash hands with soap and water before leaving the laboratory.
  • Eating, drinking, smoking and applying cosmetics are strictly prohibited in the laboratory
  • Use plasticware instead of glassware, if possible
  • Use containers with secured lids for transportations and storage
